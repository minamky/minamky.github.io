What's Mina Built?

A collection of products I've designed and built — spanning mobile apps, web tools, and behavior change.

Topics to explore

Pulse

TypeScript, React Native, Supabase

Track your workouts through Strava, attach the songs that powered them, and build a ranked library of your best workout music. Share workouts, discover new tracks, and turn your fitness history into a soundtrack.

DateBase

Swift, iOS Development, Firebase

Social media app where users can discover, rank, and share date spots in real time through a personalized friends feed and a global trending feed.

Nomi

TypeScript, React Native, Supabase

Nomi helps young adults break out of repetitive social circles by forming friendships rooted in shared curiosity rather than convenience. By matching users to teach and learn from one another, the app turns mutual skill-building into vibrant connections.

TaskDump

Accessibility, TypeScript, Voice Input

TaskDump supports users with ADHD by transforming scattered thoughts into a structured to dos. Through a voice-powered AI braindump mode, the app helps users externalize their thinking and breakdown their tasks into manageable, actionable subtasks.

Second Thought

Chrome Extension, Behavior Change

With personalized reflection questions and tailored constraints on impulsive spending, Second Thought helps users practice intentional, mindful consumption.
