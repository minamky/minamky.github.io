TaskDump

Accessibility, TypeScript, Voice Input

TaskDump supports users with ADHD by transforming scattered thoughts into a structured to dos. Through a voice-powered AI braindump mode, the app helps users externalize their thinking and breakdown their tasks into manageable, actionable subtasks.

User Need + Solution

User Need

People with ADHD often struggle with task initiation and motivation. When tasks feel large and vague, the effort required to figure out where to start can become a barrier to action. Because of this, individuals with ADHD can benefit from tools that help externalize their unstructured thoughts and translate large tasks into smaller, clear, low-friction starting points.

Capture unstructured thoughts instantly

Speak naturally about everything you need to do, quickly externalizing tasks without the friction of organizing or typing.

Organize into a task list

Brain-dumped information is structured into tasks, with AI-powered features to help break down tasks into manageable subtasks.

From Our User Study

Priority Guidance

Conversational Task Clarification

Smart Task Breakdown

Break down large or vague tasks into smaller, actionable steps with AI-generated suggestions.
