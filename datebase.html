DateBase

Swift, iOS Development, Firebase

Social media app where users can discover, rank, and share date spots in real time through a personalized friends feed and a global trending feed

Discover & Search

Google Maps SDK

Extract nearby date spots in real time like cafes, parks, galleries, and more. Users can search by name and city, ensuring they always find the perfect nearby location.

Advanced User Search

Find other users by their unique username to view profiles, check favorite date spots, and connect or follow them within the app.

Rank, Review & Rate

Advanced Filters & Categories

Narrow results by activity vibe, price range, romance levels, and physicality.

Comment and Likes

Engage with the community by leaving hearts and comments on date spots and user posts.

Date Scores

Rank each date spot with an overall score, which Been lists are ordered by.

Save, Share & Plan

Been and Want to Try Lists

The Been list lets users keep track of date spots they have already visited with an optional short note or rating after each visit. The Want to Try list tracks date spots that users bookmark for future outings.

Friends Feed vs Global Feed

The Friends Feed is a personalized stream showing only the activity, reviews, photos of people in your network. The Global Feed is the publicly visible stream of all community activity.
